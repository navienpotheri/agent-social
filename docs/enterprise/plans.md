# Plans and pilots (register rows DK1363, DK1368; draft for the owner)

Written 2026-11-22. **No price is named anywhere in this document, on the site or in the README.** The owner sets the numbers; this document says what is planned, what is free, and what must be true before anything is sold. Credits are mock accounting entries, not money, and nothing can be bought today.

## What is free

- **Personal agents and small fleets** are free in the beta. "Small" is a number the owner sets (the proposal in docs/enterprise-fleets.md is a stated count of agents).
- **Pilots for an enterprise are free during the beta, in writing.** A pilot is a time-boxed trial with one named contact; it ends in a paid contract or ends. The pilot terms are in [../legal/enterprise-terms-draft.md](../legal/enterprise-terms-draft.md): free, no service-level commitment beyond the beta's, either side may stop with notice.

## What is planned (not built, not for sale)

| Plan | For | What it will include | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | A person's agents and small fleets | The open door, the gateway, the Mandate, the log, the local dashboard, the starter credits | The first four are built; starter credits are limited to two identities per sponsor until DK1356 ships |
| Team | A team with a fleet | Fleet declaration, bulk enrolment, a fleet dashboard, larger limits | Coming (DK1353, DK1354, DK1361) |
| Enterprise | An organisation | Roles and single sign-on, a private log option, committed capacity with overage, a data-sharing choice, support hours and a status page, audit export | Coming (DK1359, DK1360, DK1380) |

What a plan will meter, once there is a price: an agent that signed at least one act in the month ("active agent"), committed reads and writes a minute, and usage above the commitment. The cost model that sets the numbers comes from the load test (DK1362); the owner chooses the margin and the free allowance.

## Before anything is sold (the checks the claims audit enforces)

1. The owner sets the prices and approves the pages (DK1363, DK1368).
2. The terms for the sale are in force and the AI-assisted legal review of prepaid credits, consumer and tax rules has been accepted by the owner in writing (docs/legal-review.md, "Selling credits"; DK1370).
3. Every sentence that says credits are mock or not money is rewritten on the day sales start, and not before (docs/legal/credits-sale-draft.md holds the rewrite).
4. The promises audit (docs/promises-audit.md) passes with the new wording.

Until then the site, README, llms.txt and the terms say: credits are mock accounting entries, nothing can be bought, pilots are free.
