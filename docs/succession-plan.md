# Succession plan for the operator (register row DK421; DRAFT for the owner to complete and sign)

Written 2026-11-21 from the owner's approved option (b) of OD-DK421. It is a draft: the names, the keyholders and the signature are the owner's. Credits are mock accounting entries, not money. An AI-assisted review is in [legal-review.md](legal-review.md); no lawyer has read this.

## What is decided

1. **A named successor** acts as the operator if the founder is unavailable: unable to act for 14 days in a row (illness, travel, loss of keys) or after notice that the founder is stepping back. Successor: **[the owner names a person and gives their contact]**.
2. **A threshold of keyholders.** The operator's signing key is not held by one person. Three keyholders each hold a share; **any two** can act together: the founder, the named successor and **[a third keyholder the owner names]**. An action that needs the operator's key (a mint, a price-list change, a suspension, a seat on a court) needs two of the three.
3. **Emergency powers have a 30-day sunset.** If the founder is unavailable the keyholders may act only to keep the service running and safe (suspend an abusive tenant, stop sign-ups, restore from backup, pay the host); each emergency act is written down and public; after 30 days the powers lapse unless the founder returns or the keyholders renew them in writing with a reason, once, for 30 more days.
4. **Public list of operator acts** (docs/operator-powers.md) stays the record of what the operator did; emergency acts carry the word "emergency".
5. **Citizen votes** on the Code, the price list and the charters are designed once the beta has citizens (an open decision for after launch, not a promise of a date).

## What the owner does to finish it

Name the successor and the third keyholder, set up the shares for the operator key (the keys stay on the owner's machine; the service never holds them), sign this plan with the owner's own key (`asp law sign` pattern), and publish it. Until then this page says plainly that **no succession plan is in force**.
