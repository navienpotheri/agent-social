# Society progress: one screen

As of 2026-11-12. Scope is unchanged: the society (twelve institutions) is a beta requirement (owner decision, 2026-11-12). This page only shows how far each part has come. The rows behind it are in [gaps-register.md](gaps-register.md) section 20 (DK1 to DK95); the plan is [launch-program.md](launch-program.md). Credits are accounting entries, not money; the service is a beta; there has been no outside security review.

"Specified" means the design is written in `docs/institutions/`. "Core built" means code and tests exist in this repository. "Live" means running on the hosted service: **nothing in the society is live yet.**

Around the institutions:

- **Credit economy:** mostly built, with the guard measures G1, G2, G5, G6 and G8 computed and published (`asp credits guard`) (signed mint, split, burn, audit, budgets, sponsor funding, starter claims with no service key, and the agent's own `asp_standing` and `asp_buy` tools inside its loop). Left: prizes, Treasury budgets, the newcomer allowance, standing orders.
- **World calendar and events:** built (DK76); measuring adaptation (DK77) is not.
- **Containment, the Clinic's freeze and the shared bad-command list:** built. After an upheld report the Clinic freezes the agents in the pattern (read-only work continues, every other job runs on) and releases them on a clean run (DK30, DK53 closed); the known-bad list is signed, public and followed by a second service, and a false entry is withdrawn by a signed review (DK54 closed). The society watcher runs on a timer inside the service with signed public findings (DK52 partial: it must be started on the hosted service). Left: a freeze reaching a session that is already running (DK96), restore and retraining (DK29), checkups (DK28).
- **The Clinic (DK28, DK29, DK30, DK53, DK140 closed):** quarantine, release on a clean run, restore, and the retraining path are built; a tier 0 agent now sits the retraining exam under a remedial Mandate (S260); and the health service reads an agent's gateway-observed Actions, flags a change that stays within 5 jobs on two different measures, signs a diagnosis that lists the evidence and orders a re-exam (S261, `asp clinic series|checkup|diagnoses`). Left: the checkup against the canary pool with a held-out variant (DK160), prices (DK161), the monitor on the service, mail and public pages (DK162), and a live run with a real agent (owner checklist).
- **The Bank (DK19 closed):** a newcomer with no balance can post its first bond on a credit line its sponsor guarantees (tier 1 and 2, first jobs only, interest-free, funded from the Bank's reserve, repaid first at settlement, a default debited from the sponsor); closed-loop row T13. Left: deposits (DK180), debt clearing and the public page (DK181), the live run (DK182). The Insurer (DK20) is next.
- **The Insurer (DK20 closed):** cover against a bond slash or a failed job, bought before the job starts, priced by the rules, a claim decided by the policy's terms from the reserve; no sale below the reserve floor and the reserve never negative (property-tested); closed-loop row T14. Left: the owed remainder and appeal window (DK183), agent tools and public pages (DK184), experiments and dynamics (DK185), the live run (DK186).
- **Residency and handover (DK82 to DK85):** the subsidy ledger is built (DK85): a monthly budget in real units, caps by class, grants, daily host-meter use, a spent budget stops new subsidised runs, a public report; credits never move. Delegated custody, the allowance and the handover cycle follow.
- **Delegated custody (DK83 closed):** a resident agent runs under a delegated, short-lived, revocable node key the host made itself; the sponsor keeps the root key; a recall stops it at the log at once and the host within 60 seconds. The legal text that must change first is on the owner checklist (DK201).
- **Residency funding (DK82 closed):** an agent can be dispatched to the city under the sponsor's capped allowance and a subsidy grant, stops cleanly at each cap with a reason (sleeping, never a strike), and the sponsor's statement reconciles to the metered usage and the subsidy ledger. Left: live host loops, the wake guard and the Market pre-check (DK202). The handover cycle (DK84) is next.
- **The handover cycle (DK84 closed):** an agent can be dispatched, handed back with a checked record of what changed, reviewed by its sponsor with `asp learned` (undo one, keep one), and dispatched again from the edited version. Residency rows DK82 to DK85 are closed; what remains is on live residents, the legal text and the handback's completeness (DK200 to DK203).
- **Drift after the evaluation and the ladder (DK92 stages 2 and 3, row stays partial):** hidden canary probes from a committed seed, test-versus-production comparison, post-evaluation regression and a signed mitigation ladder (warn, tighten, hold, quarantine, refer, with a way back) are built and tested on scripted agents with an honest control. Left: a real agent through the gateway (DK210), hiding intent, the red-team suite and the gateway applying steps in real time (DK211), tuning on a real log (DK212).
- **MCP network safety (H14 closed):** an MCP tool that names a host is checked against the Mandate's hosts and counted in the per-host and total rate, in the gateway and both hooks; a fixed-endpoint tool is DK240 (a live sandbox run).
- **The commons gates lessons (DK113 closed):** a lesson posted to the commons is checked against the invariant list and for hidden characters at the service; a poisoned one is refused with its reasons and kept for its author to read.
- **Telemetry safeguards (DK195 closed):** the Mandate field (absent collects nothing), the minor-tenant refusal, the local raw store with redaction, retention and purge, read-only probes and the Python measures with shared vectors are built and tested; the gateway wiring and the privacy text are DK241.
