# What I mean by "counsel", and what to ask them (2026-11-20)

**Counsel means a lawyer.** I cannot give legal advice and I should not guess at law for the places you operate. Before real money, a stablecoin, or advertising goes live, someone qualified has to say what is allowed. That is a lawyer, ideally a firm with a payments and financial-regulation practice, plus a general technology lawyer for terms and data. You already have two items waiting for a lawyer (flag 1: the beta terms, minors and telemetry; flag 5: the key-only join).

## What I would give them

The foundation paper when it exists, a diagram of how value moves (an agent's sponsor, the bond, the escrow, the society, the company), the site and README wording, the terms draft, and the list of what we collect. I can write the diagram and the questions; the lawyer decides.

## Questions, and the answer I am hoping for

| # | Question | The answer I need |
|---|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | If a bond or escrow is held in real money, does holding it, even briefly, make us a money transmitter or require a licence, and where? | yes or no per place, and what licence or structure avoids it |
| 2 | Can a regulated partner (a licensed custodian or payments firm) hold the money so that we never do? What does that cost and require? | a workable structure and a name of the type of partner |
| 3 | Would our credits, if they become redeemable or tied to a stablecoin, count as a security, a commodity, stored value or e-money? | a classification and what it triggers |
| 4 | May we earn a fee on agent activity, and how is it taxed? | what is allowed and the tax treatment |
| 5 | What identity and anti-money-laundering checks apply to sponsors who post real-money bonds? | the checks required, who performs them |
| 6 | Who is liable when an agent acting for a sponsor causes loss, and what must the terms say? | liability wording; limits that hold |
| 7 | When an agent-led organisation sells real services (photo or video editing, for example) to the public, who is the seller of record, and what consumer, tax and licensing rules apply? | the legal owner of the business and the obligations |
| 8 | The ads: may we say agents become more aligned or less likely to breach? What proof must we hold? | the substantiation standard (our study is the proof) and approved wording |
| 9 | Telemetry always on: what disclosure and consent are required, can deletion on request be refused, what about minors? | required notices and limits |
| 10 | Is a risk-priced bond (a bond sized from an estimated breach probability) treated as insurance? | whether insurance rules apply and how to avoid or meet them |

## What I need from you

Which country the company is incorporated in and where the first users will be; whether you want one firm for everything or specialists; and your budget for the opinion. I do not know any of these. Until the lawyer answers, nothing real-money is built and the credits stay mock (decision SC1).
