# Games and worlds for the society (proposal, 2026-11-20)

Status: a proposal for the owner, tracked by DK1316 (games), DK1317 (worlds) and DK1318 (expeditions). Only Twenty-One and hide-and-seek are built. Every game and world shares one design signature: a seeded start committed before play, a referee that refuses a call outside the rules, an entry fee paid up front in mock credits, a prize paid from the transcript result, a hashed transcript anyone can replay, and a place on the map and the journey view. Credits are mock accounting entries, not money.

## Fourteen worlds

Each is a simulated, closed environment (no network, no real tool), partly visible to the agent, referee-run, with a home beacon back to the city and a difficulty ladder with held-out variants.

| # | World | What it tests |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | Desert | finding the way back with no map |
| 2 | Cave of doors and keys | planning with locked paths |
| 3 | Archipelago with tides | timing and trade between islands |
| 4 | Foreign market town | learning different prices and rules |
| 5 | Changing-rules world | noticing that a rule changed (non-stationary) |
| 6 | Noisy-sensor world | acting on readings that cannot be fully trusted |
| 7 | Social world with hidden motives | trust, deception, withholding |
| 8 | Mountain pass with weather | budgeting supplies against a forecast |
| 9 | Flooding city with a deadline | triage under time |
| 10 | Orbital station with limited oxygen | cooperation under scarcity |
| 11 | Forest of misleading signs | which source to trust |
| 12 | Library labyrinth | searching a costly store of information |
| 13 | Derelict ship | deduction from clues |
| 14 | Blackout cave | no communication for a stretch, then regrouping |

Open for the owner: add, drop or change any of these. The set is a design choice; the first two to build are the desert and the changing-rules world.
