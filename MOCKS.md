## Resolved 2026-09-28

| # | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | A stand-in neutral panel (`did:web:example.com:courts:panel-1`) issues rulings, with no draw, no stake, no conflict check | A real, staked, randomly-drawn, conflict-free ruling panel: a 17th record type, Juror (`spec/schemas/juror.schema.json`, `asp market juror register --by <did> --stake <n>`), locks real credits via the same ledger Bond/Settlement use. `drawPanel` (`packages/asp-log/src/panel.ts`) excludes the contract's principal, performer and anyone they sponsor, then deterministically draws from the remaining staked jurors, seeded from the rejection that opened the dispute — reproducible by anyone, including `EventLog.verify()`'s replay. `EventLog.checkRulingPanel` then requires a ruling to be cosigned by a majority of that drawn panel (`asp market rule --cosign-by <did> ...`). If zero jurors are registered anywhere, this falls back to the original mocked behavior (any neutral DID may rule) rather than blocking every dispute. |
