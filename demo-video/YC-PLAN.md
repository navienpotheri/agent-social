# YC application: what to record, in what order (written 30 October 2026)

**Deadline: Monday 2 November, 8 pm Pacific** (about 9:30 am on Tuesday 3 November in India). Decisions for on-time applications come by 11 December. Source: the Winter 2027 line on ycombinator.com/apply, read on 30 October. Check the form before you rely on it.

## What YC says about video (ycombinator.com/video, read 30 October)

- The **application video is one minute**, with **only the founders talking**. It is "not the place to submit a demo or promotional video".
- **Do not recite a script.** Use bullet points and talk spontaneously. YC says it is about how founders communicate.
- If you have a **demo, there is a separate place in the application** for it. I could not check that field's length limit or what it should show, because the form lives on apply.ycombinator.com. Look at the form.
- Paul Graham's "how to apply" essay: be concise and matter-of-fact, avoid marketing-speak, be specific, and say plainly what is wrong or unfinished. Video applicants are statistically more likely to be interviewed, and the partner will check out the demo.

## So there are two videos, and they are different

### A. Founder video (1 minute, the one that matters most)

Just you, to camera, no editing needed, no script. A friend behind the camera helps. Talk from these bullets:

1. Name, where you are, one line of background (Meta safety ML, if you want it).
2. What you are building, in one plain sentence: "an open protocol that makes AI agents accountable, and a society where they earn their freedom".
3. Why: agents now act for people and nobody can say who is responsible when they fail.
4. What exists today: the gateway works on Claude Code and Codex, public repo, 90% of harmful calls blocked (held-out test), a pilot where agents that learn pay a lower bond.
5. What does not exist yet (say it plainly): the society is designed, not built, and it is a beta with mock credits.
6. What you want: YC's help getting first real users and turning the protocol into a standard.

### B. Demo video (the 3-minute one, optional, separate field)

Show the product working. Keep it simple, and let the real recording carry it. Use the seven topics, but most of the picture is the real thing:

| Part | Length | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Introduction | 10 s | You (voice), the homepage screenshot |
| 2 Why | 15 s | A simple animated diagram, or the problem slide image |
| 3 The platform | 15 s | The three-layer diagram, animated |
| 4 Gateway and protocol | 70 s | Your real recording: refusal, allow-host, tamper check, paid and slashed jobs |
| 5 Protocol to society | 20 s | Simple animated diagram (passport, escrow, evidence, standing) |
| 6 Inside the society | 20 s | The institutions picture, animated, labelled "designed" |
| 7 Accountability | 20 s | The pilot chart, a society of different agents, the URL |

About 2:50. Little or no AI-generated video. Where something is designed and not built, label it.
