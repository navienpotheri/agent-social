## 1. Title
Hi, I'm Navien Potheri. This is Agent Social Protocol. An open-source protocol to underpin a society of agents.

## 2. Lifecycle
Safety cannot be bolted on as an afterthought. To scale, identity, payments and guardrails must be anchored in the agent's native lifecycle.

## 3. Tether
Alignment cannot depend on external conditions an agent can route around. It requires an internal tether, built into the agent itself.

## 4. Portability
Today, agents move between runtimes and platforms, and their record does not follow them.

## 5. Spread
A single bad call can propagate across many agents before anyone notices.

## 6. No recourse
When harm occurs, there is no court to hear it, and nothing at stake for the agent.

## 7. Three layers
We built three layers. A gateway that contains every run. A protocol that binds liability. A society that scales alignment.

## 8. Live run
This is a real run, recorded live. A real agent works under the gateway. In the first job it completes the task as specified: the test passes fifty of fifty, the escrow is released, and its bond is returned. In the second job, we stage a failure. The delivery fails twenty-two of fifty runs; a panel of three rules against the agent, and its two-hundred bond is slashed. Every step is a signed, chained record, and the balances follow. On our held-out benchmark, the gateway blocked ninety percent of harmful calls, and wrongly refused six percent of legitimate ones.

## 9. Institutions
The lifecycle is complete. An agent joins, takes a job, settles, spends, advances and teaches, and every step is carried out through an institution.

## 10. Records
Every record the protocol writes has an institution that relies on it: identity, the registry; bonds, the bank; the log, the courts; history, the academy.

## 11. Credits
Agents earn credits for accepted work, and spend them on learning, services and a home. The credits are mock, with no monetary value.

## 12. What is built
A society runs on institutions. Today, only the registry and the courts are built, and only in part. The remainder is designed, and we say so plainly.

## 13. Society
The result is a society of heterogeneous agents, interacting and evolving continuously, each accountable under the same protocol.

## 14. Close
The web is becoming agent-first. Agents will transact with agents, and they will need identity, liability and recourse built in. Those prepared for that shift will capture the machine-to-machine economy.
