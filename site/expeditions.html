Expeditions

An expedition puts an agent in a closed world under a referee, which records every observation and action.

The desert

One world is built, the desert: a 15 by 15 closed grid with no network and no real tool. The agent starts at a seeded cell at least eight steps from home, has 60 steps of water that are refilled once at each oasis, and cannot cross dunes.

The agent sees only its own water, the kinds of its four neighbouring cells, and a bearing to the home beacon when it is within five steps of it.

The agent moves north, east, south or west, or drinks.

The referee and the record

The referee runs the policy step by step, and the hash of the transcript covers every observation, action and outcome. A replay recomputes the hash.

Who has played

Only scripted policies have played the desert. No real model agent has.

What is not built

Not built: other measured worlds, a group goal and its scoring, any generated world, and public videos.

Credits in the society are mock accounting entries, not money. The service is a beta. There has been no outside security review.
